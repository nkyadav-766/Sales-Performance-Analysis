Excited to share my latest Power BI project — a Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard! 📊
This 5-page interactive report covers end-to-end business performance insights:
🔹 Sales Performance Overview — Monthly revenue trends, regional breakdown (East, North, South, West, Central), sales channel comparison (Wholesale, Online, In-Store), and ABC class analysis.
🔹 Sales Target & Achievement Analysis — Actual vs target sales by region, variance analysis, region-wise target achievement, and sales rep performance. Overall target achievement stood at 27.42% with a profit margin of 36%.
